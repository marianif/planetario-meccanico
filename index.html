    What is computed

    Planetary positions come from Keplerian elements referred to J2000 with per-century rates, including the great Jupiter–Plute inequality. A Newton–Raphson solver iterates the eccentric anomaly. The Moon and Sun use truncated ELP2000 and solar series; sidereal time, the Ascendant and Midheaven are spherical trigonometry.

    What is drawn

    Every surface is generated at load from value noise evaluated on the unit sphere, so the maps are seamless and honest to latitude. Spheres are mapped with the relations dlon = asin(x/R), dlat = asin(y/R). The day/night line is the projection of a great circle, solved in closed form rather than faked with a gradient. Saturn's rings are the conic their own spin axis implies, built from the real ring structure.

    What is claimed

    Nothing here is a horoscope. The astronomy is real and verifiable. The astrology is four thousand years of a different kind of observation — that it is beautiful is not evidence that it is true, and it is offered as the latter, never the former.

    Keplerian elements after Standish · lunar and solar series after Meeus · radii, rotation and satellite counts after the NASA/JPL planetary fact sheets. No frameworks, no build step, no canvas libraries.
